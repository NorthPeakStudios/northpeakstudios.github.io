    How the review grades moves

    Each position is analysed by Stockfish to the review depth. A move’s centipawn loss is the mover’s evaluation before the move minus after it (evals capped at ±10 pawns; a forced mate counts as 10).

    • ★ Best: the engine’s own top move
    • ! Excellent: 0–20 cp
    • ✓ Good: 21–50 cp
    • ?! Inaccuracy: 51–100 cp
    • ? Mistake: 101–300 cp
    • ?? Blunder: over 300 cp

    To keep a won game from being covered in red, a move is only an Inaccuracy, Mistake or Blunder if it also costs at least 10, 20 or 30 points of win chance; otherwise it’s graded Good.

    Accuracy uses the lichess formula: each move is scored from the drop in win chance, then the game’s moves are combined with a volatility-weighted mean and a harmonic mean.
