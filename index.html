Courses

400 courses, from high school to doctor level.

50 courses each in Medicine, Medicinal Chemistry, Pharmacology and Neurology, plus 20 deep dives of 50 lessons each: anatomy, histology, hematology, embryology, molecular biology, neuroscience methods, and medical German, Spanish and Latin. Every lesson has a test.

Flashcards

Rate a card. Synapse schedules the next review.

Tap Again, Hard, Good or Easy and the card comes back right before you'd forget it. Your home screen shows how many are due and how long they take.

Doctordle

A new patient to diagnose every day.

Read the case, guess the condition. Each wrong guess reveals the next clue: history, vitals, exam, labs, then imaging. Six tries, then the full explanation.

The Lab

Run a code blue. Read a CT. Name the rhythm.

30 tools that turn what you study into practice: patient sims, lab panels, vitals, a lesion locator, an ECG trainer and a Drug Lab.

App 2 · Axon

Your notes, linked like your brain.

Write in Markdown, link ideas with [[double brackets]] and watch them connect. Everything lives on your device first.

Notes

Type [[ and link one idea to the next.

Every note shows its links and backlinks, so a page on dopamine leads straight to Parkinson’s, levodopa and the basal ganglia. Folders, tags, search, checklists and Markdown are built in.

Graph

See how everything you know connects.

The graph draws your notes as a map, colored by folder. Tap a note to see its neighbors, tap again to open it, pinch to zoom.
