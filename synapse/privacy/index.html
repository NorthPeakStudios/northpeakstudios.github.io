Privacy policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Synapse is made by Northpeak Studios. This policy explains what the app stores and what it does not. The short version: we store only what your account and sync need, and we never sell it.

What is stored

  • Your cards and progress (decks, review history, settings and preferences) are saved on your device, inside the app's own storage.
  • Optional account. If you create an account, we store your email address and an encrypted (hashed) password, plus your study data (decks, review history, stats, streak, course progress and settings) so it syncs to every device you sign in on. Synced data travels over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. We also store whether the account has a purchase (Synapse Lifetime or Northpeak All-Access) and when its free trial started. We never store or see your password itself.
  • Free trial. Without an account, a random ID for your device and the date its trial started are stored so the 3-day trial runs once.
  • Buying Synapse Lifetime or Northpeak All-Access. The name and phone number you type in the purchase form are stored so we can send the Apple Cash request and your unlock code.
  • Optional sync code. If you turn on sync, the app creates a private sync code. Your cards and progress are copied to our sync storage under that code so you can open them on another device. Anyone with the code can load that data, so keep it private. You can stop syncing at any time, and you can ask us to delete synced data (see Contact).

What we do not do

  • An account is optional. The only personal information the app asks for is an email if you create an account, and a name and phone number if you buy.
  • No ads.
  • No tracking, analytics profiles or third-party trackers.
  • We never sell or share your data.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app under Settings → Account → Delete account. This deletes your email, password hash and synced progress from our server right away. Data on your device is removed when you clear it in the app's settings or uninstall the app. To delete data saved under a sync code, email us with the code and we will remove it.

Children

Synapse is not directed at children under 13. Apart from the optional account email and the optional purchase phone number, it does not ask for personal information.

Medical content

Synapse is for learning only, not medical advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
