Synapse

Flashcards and medicine study that actually stick.

Try the web version Coming soon toGoogle Play

What it does

Synapse combines spaced-repetition flashcards with courses, hands-on study tools and language practice, so one app covers a whole study routine.

Spaced repetition

Cards come back right before you would forget them, so review time goes where it counts.

Courses

Structured lessons that walk you through a topic step by step, then turn it into cards.

The Lab

Medical and science study tools: an ECG trainer, a patient simulator, a vitals interpreter, 3D anatomy and more.

Languages

Practice German, French and Spanish with cards and speaking drills.

Works offline

Install it to your home screen and keep studying without a connection.

No accounts, no ads

Your cards and progress live on your device. Sync between devices with a private sync code if you want.

Screenshots

For learning only, not medical advice. Synapse is a study tool. Always talk to a doctor or pharmacist about real health questions.
