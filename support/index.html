Support

How can we help?

Answers to common questions about Synapse. Still stuck? Email northpeak.support@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

How much does Synapse cost?

Every new user gets 3 days of full access for free. After that it is $35 one time for Synapse Lifetime, or $100 one time for Northpeak All-Access, which also includes every future Northpeak app. No subscription.

How do I buy?

In the app, tap Pay with Apple Cash and enter your name and phone number. You get an Apple Cash request for the amount within 24 hours. After you pay, your unlock code arrives; enter it under Have an unlock code?

Do I need an account?

No. You can start the trial without one. With a free account (email and password) your decks, progress, streak and purchase sync to every device you sign in on.

How do I move my study to another device?

Sign in to the same account on the other device. Everything syncs automatically.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Open Synapse, add it to your home screen, and it keeps working without a connection.

I paid but have no code yet.

Codes are sent after the Apple Cash payment arrives, usually within 24 hours. If it has been longer, email us with the name and phone number you used.

Is the medical content advice?

No. Synapse is for learning only, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or pharmacist about real health questions.

I found a bug or have an idea.

Email northpeak.support@gmail.com. Screenshots help a lot.
