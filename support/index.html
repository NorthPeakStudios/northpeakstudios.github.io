Support

How can we help?

Answers to common questions about Synapse. Still stuck? Email us.

Is Synapse free?

Yes. The web version is free to use, with no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts. Your cards and progress are saved on your device.

How do I move my cards to another device?

Turn on sync in the app to get a private sync code, then enter that code on your other device. Keep the code to yourself, since anyone with it can load your cards.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Open the web version, add it to your home screen, and it keeps working without a connection.

When is the Google Play version coming?

It is being prepared now. This page will link to it as soon as it is live.

Is the medical content advice?

No. Synapse is for learning only, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or pharmacist about real health questions.

I found a bug or have an idea. Where do I send it?

Email northpeak.support@gmail.com. Screenshots help a lot.
