Odin Cards

Privacy policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

Odin Cards is made by Northpeak Studios. This policy explains what the app stores and what it does not. The short version: your study data stays with you.

What is stored

  • Your cards and progress (decks, review history, settings and preferences) are saved on your device, inside the app's own storage.
  • Optional sync. If you turn on sync, the app creates a private sync code. Your cards and progress are copied to our sync storage under that code so you can open them on another device. Anyone with the code can load that data, so keep it private. You can stop syncing at any time, and you can ask us to delete synced data (see Contact).

What we do not do

  • No accounts. You never sign up or give us your name, email or phone number to use the app.
  • No ads.
  • No tracking, analytics profiles or third-party trackers.
  • We never sell or share your data.

Deleting your data

Data on your device is removed when you clear it in the app's settings or uninstall the app. To delete data saved under a sync code, email us with the code and we will remove it.

Children

Odin Cards does not ask for personal information from anyone, including children.

Medical content

Odin Cards is for learning only, not medical advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
